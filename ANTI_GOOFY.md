# Anti-Goofy Checklist

These are concrete things that felt wrong in Half Sword. This list is the
acceptance test for every phase and the "are we done?" criterion.
At the end of each phase, report which items are solved, partially
solved, or not yet testable.

## Sword behavior
- [ ] The blunt/flat part of the blade hits something when I clearly
      intended an edge or tip hit.
- [ ] The sword moves too slowly in some swings for no clear reason
      (inconsistent speed / unpredictable sluggishness).
- [ ] I aim at a spot and the sword passes beside it instead of hitting.
- [ ] A stab I clearly intended ends up going sideways.

## Arms and body
- [ ] Arms bend or twist into impossible/unnatural poses.
- [ ] The sword goes to a place the arms cannot anatomically reach.
- [ ] Walking looks slippery, floaty, or comical.

## Parry
- [ ] A correct-looking parry intent (e.g. pulling the sword across while
      the enemy attacks) fails because of a small wrist-angle error.
- [ ] Sword control feels "not where I put it", with no margin for error
      (too unforgiving).

## Overall feeling
- [ ] I get the feeling "I didn't intend that" after an action.
- [ ] Freedom exists, but absurd outcomes do not.

## Things I want to KEEP from Half Sword
- Weight and momentum of the sword
- Freedom to choose angle and timing
- Physical, believable clashes
